Documents

Document intake and routing

Staff manually sort emailed forms, invoices, and attachments.

Explore the workflow ↓

Possible approach

Classify documents and route draft records for review.

For example

A finance team checks extracted invoice details before entry.

Human review

Staff approve uncertain fields and protect customer records.

What to measure

Track handling time, corrections, and routing exceptions.

Customers

Customer query triage

Repeated questions delay replies to complex customer needs.

Explore the workflow ↓

Possible approach

Group queries and suggest replies from approved information.

For example

Staff review draft answers about delivery or returns.

Human review

Staff own complaints, data requests, and uncertain cases.

What to measure

Track response time, corrections, and unresolved queries.
