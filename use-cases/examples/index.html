Four situations to discuss with your team. Each starts with the work, the information it needs and the person who checks the result.

These are hypothetical examples adapted from our earlier illustrative case studies. They contain no customer testimonials or achieved savings.

01 / Accountancy

Document collection in an accountancy practice

Illustrative situation

The situation

Staff repeatedly request missing client records.

A possible change

Shared tracking; staff approve drafted reminders.

What it needs

Agree documents, contacts, owners, and access.

Where a person stays involved

Staff approve messages and resolve exceptions.

How to assess value

Track time, responses, corrections, and open requests.

02 / Legal services

Status updates for conveyancing matters

Illustrative situation

The situation

Staff answer repeat enquiries across matter records.

A possible change

Summarise milestones into staff-reviewed updates.

What it needs

Agree milestones, access, sharing rules, and owners.

Where a person stays involved

Legal staff verify facts before sending updates.

How to assess value

Track preparation time, repeat enquiries, and corrections.

03 / Healthcare administration

Administrative recall coordination

Illustrative situation

The situation

Staff reconcile administrative lists across systems.

A possible change

Review one list and prepare approved invitations.

What it needs

Agree cohort, wording, access, preferences, escalation.

Where a person stays involved

Administrative only; clinicians own clinical decisions.

How to assess value

Track reconciliation, invitation status, handling, exceptions.

04 / Small business operations

Event close-out and invoice administration

Illustrative situation

The situation

Event close-out details span inboxes and spreadsheets.

A possible change

Shared checklist prepares invoice requests for review.

What it needs

Agree records, sign-off, invoice fields, and owners.

Where a person stays involved

Staff verify details before invoice creation.

How to assess value

Track timing, missing details, corrections, open tasks.
