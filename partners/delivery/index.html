Where does the data sit, and where is the work delivered?＋

Where the team works and where your data is handled are agreed with you in writing before work starts. We’re glad to work UK business hours. Where possible we work inside your repo, cloud accounts and tooling, so access stays under your control. A data processing agreement is available.

Who talks to the client?＋

You decide. Under your name, you lead the client relationship and we work in the background, with our role disclosed as your data protection contract requires. Co-branded, we can join client calls. Either way, you own the relationship.

Who owns the IP?＋

Client interfaces, commercial terms, project IP and reusable components are agreed in writing before work starts. The partner terms above summarise the usual position; the signed agreement governs.

What size of work do you take on?＋

Bounded pieces first: scoping, then a pilot of two to three weeks. Larger builds follow only if the pilot earns them.

Do you compete with us?＋

We build for you, not around you. What we agree on approaching your clients, and for how long, is set out in the written agreement.
