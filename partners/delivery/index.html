01 / Warehouse AI

AI apps over a data warehouse

An assistant that answers approved business questions from the client’s own warehouse. Metrics are defined first, results are traceable, access is controlled and answers are tested against known figures.

Example first piece

A scoped pilot on one dataset and a short list of agreed questions.

02 / Protected data

PII redaction and validation on incoming data

A boundary that detects and redacts personal data, validates what arrives and holds exceptions and uncertain matches for review before data moves on to the warehouse or an AI model. Redaction is tested on sample data and the acceptable error rate is agreed with you at scoping.

Example first piece

One incoming feed, with a working redaction and validation step.

03 / Data engineering

Data engineering and production readiness

Pipelines, orchestration (Airflow) and warehouse ingestion, plus the work that takes an existing pipeline, RAG app or prototype to production: evaluation, monitoring, deployment and operating notes.

Example first piece

A production-readiness review of one existing build, with a prioritised fix list.

Option A

Under your name.

We work as part of your team. The client sees your brand and your people in front. Our role as processor or sub-processor, and the delivery location, are disclosed to your client as your data protection contract requires.

Option B

Co-branded.

We are introduced by name where that helps the client, and you still lead the relationship.

Agreed before work starts

  • You keep the client and the relationship. We work as your subcontractor; client interfaces and commercial terms are agreed with you before work starts.
  • We do not approach your clients to offer them our services directly. The exact scope and period are set out in the agreement.
  • Confidentiality first. We sign a mutual confidentiality agreement before you share client or project detail with us.
  • Ownership. Bespoke deliverables pass to you or your client on payment, as agreed. We keep pre-existing tools and reusable components, licensed for use with the deliverables.
  • Data protection. Personal data is handled under a DPA, with Quiet Staff as processor or sub-processor. Where India delivery involves a restricted transfer, the UK IDTA or Addendum applies.
  • In writing. All of this is confirmed in a written agreement, signed before work starts.

This page summarises how Quiet Staff (QuietStaff Ltd, company 17171308) usually works with delivery partners. It is not an offer or a contract, and it creates no obligation. Only a written agreement signed by both parties does, and that agreement governs.

04Partner questions

Before you
hand anything over.

Where does the data sit, and does India-based delivery matter?＋

Delivery is from India with agreed UK overlap hours. Where possible we work inside your repo, cloud accounts and tooling, so access stays under your control. A data processing agreement is available.

Who talks to the client?＋

You decide. Under your name, you lead the client relationship and we work in the background, with our role disclosed as your data protection contract requires. Co-branded, we can join client calls. Either way, you own the relationship.

Who owns the IP?＋

Client interfaces, commercial terms, project IP and reusable components are agreed in writing before work starts. The partner terms above summarise the usual position; the signed agreement governs.

What size of work do you take on?＋

Bounded pieces first: scoping, then a pilot of two to three weeks. Larger builds follow only if the pilot earns them.

Do you compete with us?＋

We build for you, not around you. What we agree on approaching your clients, and for how long, is set out in the written agreement.
