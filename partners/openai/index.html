01Our partnership

The OpenAI Partner Network recognises organisations that build and deliver AI solutions for businesses. Select Partner is one tier within that network. As part of it, we help organisations adopt AI responsibly and effectively.

We are developing Quiet Staff One, an Agent-as-a-Service product for small businesses. Alongside product development, we offer scoped AI advice, workflow implementation, integrations and support. The self-service product is in development; each engagement has its own agreed scope.

02What this means for you

What this means for you

  • A defined starting point — we agree the workflow, intended outcome and responsibilities before work begins.
  • Human oversight — we agree review and approval points for the workflow, including where a person must decide.
  • Scoped support — maintenance, monitoring and changes are included only where agreed for the engagement.
