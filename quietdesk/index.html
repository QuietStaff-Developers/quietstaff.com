QuietDesk / For heating and plumbing firms

Enquiries answered.
Jobs followed up.

QuietDesk answers enquiries from your website chat, your web form and forwarded email. It asks the job questions, offers a visit slot you confirm, and drafts quotes from your own price sheet for you to approve.

Try the live demo ↓See prices ↓

The demo is a made-up firm, Bramholt Heating and Plumbing.

01How it works

A customer asks.
You stay in charge.

  1. A customer gets in touch

    By chat, by form or by forwarded email.

  2. QuietDesk asks the job questions

    What the job is, boiler make, postcode, how urgent it is and when suits.

  3. You confirm the visit

    It offers a slot and you approve it.

  4. You approve the quote

    It is drafted from your price sheet and chased on day 2, 5 and 10.

02What you get

What you get,
in plain terms.

At launch

  • Website chat.
  • Web form.
  • Forwarded email.
  • Visit slot offers that you confirm.
  • Quotes drafted from your price sheet for you to approve.
  • Chasing on day 2, 5 and 10.

Quotes, visit confirmations and chasing are switched on for each firm during onboarding, once the firm has accepted the QuietDesk terms.
