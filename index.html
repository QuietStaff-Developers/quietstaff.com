Before we talk

A few useful
things to know.

Do we need any AI experience?

No. Bring a task that takes too much time, the tools you use and your questions. We will guide the discussion in plain language.

How does the appointment work?

We arrange a one-hour remote call, primarily on Google Meet, through WhatsApp or email. Payment is due before the appointment. Colleagues from your company are welcome to join.

Will we see a demo or get an automation?

We may show relevant examples from existing work or our own setup. A bespoke demo, live build or deployed automation is not included in QuietStart.

What should we share?

A description of your workflow is enough to start. Please do not send passwords or sensitive customer, patient or employee information. We can discuss appropriate handling before reviewing business data. See our Privacy Policy.
