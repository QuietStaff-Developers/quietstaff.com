Logistics & operations / Workflow idea

A clearer handoff for every request.

A workflow idea for an operations team coordinating enquiries and status updates.

01The everyday challenge

Make the next step
easier to review.

Requests can become fragmented across inboxes and systems. Each handoff needs the right reference, context and owner.

Connected modules · Quiet Desk + Quiet Flow

02A possible workflow

  1. 01

    Capture a request with its shipment or job reference.

  2. 02

    Prepare the relevant context from approved sources.

  3. 03

    Escalate missing information and exceptions to a person.

  4. 04

    Draft the next update and record the approved action.

The boundaries matter

Time-sensitive exceptions need a named human owner. System connections and any permission to send updates would be agreed before implementation.
