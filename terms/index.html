2. What a Quiet Build is

  • One session, one automation. A Quiet Build is a one-hour live session over video call, in which we set up one workflow automation for your business (the "Build").
  • The scope is written down. Before the session, or at its start, we confirm the Build in writing, usually by email: what it will do, which tools it uses, and what "working" means. That written note is the scope. Anything not in it is not included.
  • No surprise charges. If the Build turns out to need more than the session, we will tell you and either reduce it to something we can finish in the session or quote separately for the rest. We never charge more without your written agreement.
  • Bigger work is separate. More automations, ongoing support or larger projects need their own written agreement.

3. Price and payment

  • You pay the price shown on the payment page, in full, by card, before the session. Card payments are processed by Stripe.
  • We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. The price shown is the total you pay.
  • Introductory prices apply only while the introductory offer is running. If you pay an introductory price after the offer has ended, we will tell you, and you can choose either a full refund or to pay the difference up to the standard price.

4. Booking your session

  • After you pay, we send you a link to book your session. Please book within 30 days of paying. If you have not booked within 30 days, we will refund you in full on request.
  • Please join on time. The session ends at its booked time, even if it starts late.

5. What you need to provide

  • Access to the tools the Build uses, in accounts that you own or control, with any permission needed to let us work in them.
  • A decision-maker in the session who can log in and approve changes.
  • A backup of anything important in those tools before the session.

Where a tool allows it, give us a temporary or limited user rather than your own login, and remove or change our access after the session. We do not keep your logins after the session.

If the Build cannot go ahead because access, accounts or information you were due to provide are not ready, the session counts as used. We will offer to rebook it once, free, within 30 days.
