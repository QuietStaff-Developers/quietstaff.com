2. What a Quiet Build is

  • One session, one automation. A Quiet Build is a one-hour live session over video call, in which we set up one workflow automation for your business (the "Build").
  • The scope is written down. Before the session, or at its start, we confirm the Build in writing, usually by email: what it will do, which tools it uses, and what "working" means. That written note is the scope. Anything not in it is not included.
  • No surprise charges. If the Build turns out to need more than the session, we will tell you and either reduce it to something we can finish in the session or quote separately for the rest. We never charge more without your written agreement.
  • Bigger work is separate. More automations, ongoing support or larger projects need their own written agreement.

14. QuietDesk

The QuietDesk terms in this section are version qd-terms-v1. This is the version in force for QuietDesk, and the version a firm accepts before quotes or visit emails are switched on for it.

The service

QuietDesk is an enquiry-handling service for heating and plumbing firms. It handles enquiries that reach your firm through the chat on your website, a web form, or email that you forward to QuietDesk.

For each enquiry, QuietDesk can:

  • reply to the person who made the enquiry and ask for the details your firm needs;
  • offer visit slots, which you confirm;
  • draft a quote from your price sheet, which you approve;
  • chase an enquiry that has gone quiet, on day 2, 5 and 10.

You decide which visits are confirmed and which quotes are approved. Replies in the chat are written by an AI model, so section 7 of these terms and the part headed Your responsibilities apply to them.

We do not promise that QuietDesk will be available at any particular time, or that it will respond within any particular time. QuietDesk is a tool that helps your firm handle enquiries. It does not replace your own judgement or your own dealings with your customers.

Section 1 (business customers only), section 7 (third-party tools and AI) and section 13 (contact) of these terms apply to QuietDesk as well. QuietDesk is for heating and plumbing firms only.

Pilot scope

QuietDesk is in a pilot. During the pilot, QuietDesk handles enquiries for free survey visits only. A free survey visit is a visit to look at the job that your firm does not charge for. Unless a feature has been switched on for your firm as set out below:

  • QuietDesk does not draft, send or chase quotes, and does not ask for or take deposits or balance payments;
  • QuietDesk does not send visit confirmation or visit-moved emails to your customers. It records the visit times a customer prefers, and your firm arranges the visit with them; and
  • QuietDesk does not follow up an enquiry that reaches it by forwarded email, other than one automatic reply to the sender with a link to your enquiry form. Your firm follows those enquiries up itself.

Anything in this section 14 that describes quotes, chasing or visit confirmations describes what QuietDesk can do once switched on, not what it does during the pilot.

A feature outside the pilot scope is switched on for your firm only if we have told you by email that it is available and you have accepted these terms, at the version stated at the start of this section, on the Features page of your QuietDesk account. We may switch a feature off at any time where we reasonably consider it necessary for legal or safety reasons, and we will tell you. The pilot scope applies until we tell you by email that the pilot has ended. Any other change to the pilot scope takes effect only if QuietStaff confirms it to you in writing by email to the address on your account.

The pilot scope describes what QuietDesk does. It does not limit what your firm may do for its own customers outside QuietDesk, and your firm remains responsible for those dealings.

When the trial ends, your card is charged for the plan you chose, and then every month. Conversation allowance explains what counts as a conversation and what happens at the limit.

Conversation allowance

QuietDesk counts conversations. A conversation is a new chat, a submitted enquiry form, or a new enquiry email. Replies on the same chat or the same email do not count again. A conversation is counted once, when it starts.

PeriodConversations allowed

When a paid plan reaches 80 percent of its allowance, we email the firm's owner once in that billing period.

When the limit is reached (30 in the trial, or 100 or 300 on a paid plan), new enquiries receive a fixed holding reply. Conversations that are already open can finish. We email the owner once.
