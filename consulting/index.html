03Secure data platforms

Data platforms and AI that can be trusted with sensitive data.

Protected ingestion, warehouse pipelines and AI over your own data.

For companies whose data cannot leave the boundary unchecked. Mohan has architected and run protected data ingestion, with personal data redacted or encrypted at the boundary, outside the main network, and loads that stop when undeclared personal data arrives.

  • 01
    Protected data ingestion

    A boundary that detects and redacts personal data, validates what arrives and holds exceptions and uncertain matches for review before data moves on to the warehouse or an AI model.

  • 02
    Data platforms and orchestration

    Pipelines, orchestration with Airflow and warehouse ingestion for overnight processing at volume, built to run and be monitored.

  • 03
    AI over your own data

    An assistant that answers approved business questions from your warehouse. Metrics are defined first, results are traceable, access is controlled and answers are tested against known figures.
