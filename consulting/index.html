03A way to work together

Start focused.
Move forward with clarity.

01 / Discover

Bring the problem.

Tell us what happens today, what is getting in the way and what a useful change would look like.

02 / Define

Agree the engagement.

We agree deliverables, fees, timing and responsibilities before paid work begins. Advice can be a standalone engagement.

Make progress visible.

For implementation, work towards agreed acceptance criteria with review points, documentation and a clear handover.

Implementation, third-party costs and ongoing support are scoped separately. There is no obligation to continue beyond the work you agree.
